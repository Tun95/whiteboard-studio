About

Whiteboard Wave Studio . A free app to turn scripts and images into whiteboard videos, app ads and manhwa recaps.

Built on open-source software: whiteboard-animator by Masih Sultani (MIT licence), Kokoro text-to-speech (Apache-2.0), and FFmpeg.
