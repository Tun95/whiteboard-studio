About

Whiteboard Wave Studio . A free app to turn scripts and images into whiteboard videos, app ads and manhwa recaps.

Built on free, open-source software: whiteboard-animator by Masih Sultani (MIT), Kokoro AI voices (Apache-2.0), Whisper speech recognition by OpenAI through faster-whisper (MIT), Z-Image Turbo and Qwen3 (Apache-2.0) with stable-diffusion.cpp (MIT) for doodle pictures, FFmpeg, python-docx, ReportLab and the DejaVu font. All free to use with no account or key. Full list: THIRD_PARTY_LICENSES.md.
